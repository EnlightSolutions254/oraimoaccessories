Bringing genuine Oraimo tech closer to you

We're an authorized Oraimo accessories dealer based in Nairobi, on a mission to make quality tech — power banks, audio, wearables, home appliances and grooming gadgets — simple to find and easy to order anywhere in Kenya.

Why we started

Too many people were being sold counterfeit "Oraimo" accessories at markets and online — devices that died within weeks and came with zero support. We set out to fix that by becoming an authorized dealer, stocking only genuine products, and backing every order with a real warranty.

How ordering works

Browse the shop, tap Order on whatever you need to add it to your order list, and check out — no card details, no account required. Your order details are packaged into a WhatsApp message so our team can confirm stock, pricing and delivery with you directly, human to human.

Where we deliver

We dispatch orders from Nairobi to all 47 counties through trusted courier partners, with same-day options available within the city for orders placed before 2pm.

Authorized dealer

Countrywide delivery
